# Code of Conduct

Generative Loaders is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while addressing the impact of words and actions.
- Welcome people of all backgrounds and experience levels.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on the work.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person’s information without permission.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when needed to protect the community.

This policy applies in repository spaces and whenever someone is representing the project publicly.
